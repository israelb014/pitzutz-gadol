# Art contract: פיצוץ גדול (pitzutz-gadol)

Top-down maze bomb game, slight 3/4 angle. Look: clean, colourful, soft vector cartoon with dark outline, like Kenney's
"New Platformer Pack" (see ~/work/kfitsa-gdola-unity/Assets/Resources/Art for the mood). 100% ORIGINAL: nothing resembling
the classic maze-bomb games (no white round helmet hero, no antenna with pink ball, no plain black ball bomb with white shine, no existing brands).

All PNGs (RGBA) go to ~/work/pitzutz-gadol-unity/Assets/Resources/Art/ flat, lower-case names, generated by tools/make_art.py
(pycairo + PIL, drawn at 2-4x and downscaled for clean edges). Rerunnable. 96 pixels = 1 grid cell (Art.PPU = 96).
Everything must read clearly at ~80 px per cell on a phone.

## Cast
Hero "פיצי": small round mole demolition worker. Warm brown fur, big pink nose, yellow hard hat with a small lamp, blue overalls.
Bomb = a POMEGRANATE (רימון, also Hebrew for grenade): round deep red fruit with small crown on top, a short lit fuse from the crown.
Enemies (each must be a distinct silhouette):
 snail (זחלון) slow; beetle (חיפושון) normal; ghostmoth (עשן) drifts through blocks, a smoky moth; fox (שועלון) chases;
 frog (קפצן) hops over blocks; turtle (שריונן) two hits, armoured; crate mimic (ארגזון) looks like a block then opens with teeth;
 wasp (צרעה) very fast hurry-up enemy.
Bosses (big, distinct; each canvas 288x288, feet at bottom centre):
 boss1 "מלך האשפה": a garbage truck robot with an angry face, city. boss2 "עקרב החולות": giant sand scorpion, desert.
 boss3 "המכבש": factory press robot with big hydraulic fists. boss4 "לורד הגחלים": armoured ember-knight with a horned helm, fortress.
